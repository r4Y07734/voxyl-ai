How the AI is run

The chat interface is Open WebUI. Models run on Ollama and images are generated with ComfyUI, all on hardware we operate ourselves. Your messages are not sent to a third-party AI provider. We do not use your chats to train or fine-tune models. If web search is enabled, your search query is sent to the search provider.

As administrators we have technical access to the servers and databases. We do not read your chats except to fix a technical fault, to investigate a security or abuse problem, to follow the scanning rules below, or when the law requires it.

Messages to abliterated models are scanned

Abliterated models have their built-in refusals removed. To keep them from being used to cause harm, we scan the messages you send to them and the replies they produce, including image prompts sent to ComfyUI. The scanning is automated with human review of flagged items and looks for illegal content, threats and other prohibited use under the Terms of Service.

Flagged messages may be read by our team members, who can act on them: warn you, suspend your account, or report to the authorities where the law requires. Flagged items are kept for 7 days unless law requires otherwise. Other messages are not kept beyond your normal chat history.

This applies only to abliterated models. Chats with Free-tier models are not scanned. You must accept this before using an abliterated model.

How long we keep it

  • Chats and account data are kept while your account is active. You can delete any chat permanently at any time.
  • Accounts that stay inactive for 7 days are fully deleted.
  • To delete your account earlier, email us.
  • Access requests: 7 days after we decide.
  • Invoices and accounting records: as long as the law requires, normally several years.
  • Technical logs: maximum of 7 days.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or export your data, and to restrict or object to how we use it. Details and how to complain are on the GDPR page.

Cookies, security, age, changes

Cookies. This website sets no cookies of its own. The chat app uses essential cookies or local storage only to keep you logged in. We use no advertising or tracking.

Security. Data stays on servers we control and is protected with access controls and updates. No system is perfectly secure.

Age. Voxyl AI is for people aged 18 or over.

Changes. If we change this policy in a meaningful way we'll update the date above and tell account holders by email.
