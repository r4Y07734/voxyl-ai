# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: August 18, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Voxyl AI** (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use **Voxyl AI Chat** (“Service”).
By using the Service, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

We aim to collect only the information necessary to provide and operate the Service.
### Account Information
When you create an account, we may collect and store:
* Your name.
* Your email address.
* Your password.
Passwords are stored using appropriate security measures and are not stored in plain text.
### Chat Data
When you use Voxyl AI Chat, we store your AI conversations, including the messages and content you submit and the AI-generated responses associated with those conversations.
Your chats are stored on our servers so that you can access your conversation history and use the Service.
### Device and Technical Information
We may collect limited technical information associated with your use of the Service, including:
* IP address.
* Browser information.
* Device information.
This information is primarily used to maintain your login session, keep your account secure, prevent abuse, and operate the Service.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
* Create and maintain your account.
* Authenticate you and keep you logged in.
* Store and display your AI conversations.
* Provide and operate Voxyl AI Chat.
* Maintain the security of the Service.
* Detect and prevent unauthorized access, abuse, or misuse.
* Respond to support requests.
* Comply with applicable legal obligations.
We do not collect additional personal information unless it is reasonably necessary to operate the Service or required by law.

## 3. How Long We Keep Your Information

Your AI chats remain stored on our servers until you delete the relevant chats.
If you request deletion of your account, we will delete your account and associated personal information, subject to any information we are legally required or permitted to retain.
To request account deletion, contact us using the contact information provided below.
Some information may need to be retained for a limited period where necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, or protect the security of the Service.

## 4. Chat Privacy

Your AI conversations are associated with your account and stored on our servers.
You should avoid entering highly sensitive personal information into your chats unless you are comfortable with that information being processed and stored as described in this Privacy Policy.
We do not claim that any internet-connected service can provide absolute security or confidentiality.
