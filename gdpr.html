We aim to collect and process only the personal data necessary to provide and secure Voxyl AI.

When you create an account, we process:

When you use Voxyl AI Chat, we process and store:

We may process limited technical information, including:
* IP address.
* Browser information.
* Device information.
* Information necessary to maintain your authenticated session.
This information is primarily used to keep you logged in, maintain account security, prevent abuse, and operate the Service.

We process personal data for the following purposes:
* Creating and managing your account.

## 4. Legal Bases for Processing

Under Article 6 of the GDPR, we rely on different legal bases depending on the processing activity.
### Performance of a Contract
We process information necessary to provide Voxyl AI to you, including your account information and chat data, because this processing is necessary to provide the Service you requested.
This may include:
* Creating and maintaining your account.
* Authenticating your account.
* Storing your conversations.
* Providing AI chat functionality.
### Legitimate Interests
We may process certain technical information where necessary for our legitimate interests, including:
* Protecting the security of the Service.
* Preventing fraud and abuse.
* Detecting unauthorized access.
* Maintaining and improving the reliability of the Service.
* Protecting our systems and users.
When relying on legitimate interests, we consider and balance our interests against your rights and freedoms.
### Legal Obligation
We may process or retain personal data when necessary to comply with a legal obligation applicable to us.

## 5. How Long We Keep Your Data

We follow the principle of storage limitation and aim to retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected.
### AI Chats
Your AI chats remain stored on our servers until:
* You delete the relevant chats; or
* You request deletion of your account.
When you delete chats, we will delete them from our active systems, subject to any limited retention that may be necessary for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes.
### Account Information
Your account information is generally retained while your account remains active.
If you request account deletion, we will delete your account and associated personal data, subject to information that we are legally required or permitted to retain.
### Technical Information
Technical information such as IP addresses and device or browser information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including authentication, security, and abuse prevention.
Where a specific retention period cannot reasonably be determined in advance, we use the relevant purpose and necessity of the information to determine how long it should be retained.

Your personal data may be processed or stored outside the European Economic Area if required by our infrastructure or service providers.

## 9. Your GDPR Rights

Subject to the conditions and limitations established by the GDPR, you may have the following rights:
### Right of Access
You may request confirmation of whether we process your personal data and request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
### Right to Rectification
You may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
### Right to Erasure
You may request deletion of your personal data in certain circumstances.
You can also request deletion of your Voxyl AI account by contacting us.
### Right to Restriction
You may request that we restrict the processing of your personal data in certain circumstances.
### Right to Object
You may object to certain processing of your personal data where the GDPR provides you with this right.
### Right to Data Portability
Where applicable, you may request to receive personal data you provided to us in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format, or request that it be transferred to another controller.
### Right to Withdraw Consent
Where we process personal data on the basis of your consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time.
Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.
### Rights Regarding Automated Decision-Making
Where applicable, you may have rights concerning solely automated decision-making that produces legal effects or similarly significant effects concerning you.
Voxyl AI does not intend to use AI-generated responses to make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about users.

You may also contact the data protection authority in the EU/EEA country where you live, work, or where you believe an infringement occurred.
