Email us to use any of these. We reply within one month. You can delete your own chats in the app at any time. To delete your account, email us. We may ask you to confirm your identity first.

Complaints

If you think we've handled your data unlawfully, you can complain to us first, and you always have the right to complain to the supervisory authority. In Finland that is the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman (Tietosuojavaltuutetun toimisto), tietosuoja.fi.
