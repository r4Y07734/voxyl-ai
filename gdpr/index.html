GDPR

Last updated — August 22, 2026

If you're in the EU, EEA, or UK, this covers the specific rights you have over your data under GDPR (and UK GDPR), on top of what's in the privacy policy.

1. Data controller

Voxyl AI is the controller for the personal data described here — the entity that decides why and how your data is processed. Contact details are at the bottom of this page.

2. Legal basis for processing

We process your account and chat data to provide the service you signed up for (performance of a contract), and limited data like security logs under our legitimate interest in keeping Voxyl AI safe and working.

3. Your rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restrict or object to certain processing
  • Receive your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent, where processing is based on it

4. Exercising your rights

Email us and we'll respond within the timeframe GDPR requires (usually one month). We may need to verify your identity first, to make sure we're not handing your data to someone else.

5. International transfers

If your data is processed outside the EU/EEA or UK — for example by a hosting provider — we rely on approved safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses to keep it protected.

6. Data retention

We keep your data only as long as your account is active. See the privacy policy for more details.

7. Complaints

If you think we've mishandled your data, you can complain to us directly, or to the data protection authority in your country — in Finland, that's the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman.

8. Contact

For GDPR requests or questions, email ottoemil.heiskanen@proton.me.
